NASA Space Apps Vancouver

Fall 2026 · Team project

Opportunity was built to last 90 sols. It kept exploring Mars for more than 14 years.

For NASA Space Apps Vancouver, our team built two 3D browser games. In Last Light, you drive Opportunity, look for evidence of ancient water, and send your findings back to Earth.

The discoveries are something you work out yourself. At one site, you trace the layers in a rock to figure out how it formed. The game pairs those moments with real NASA images and an original score that changes as you explore. We wanted you to feel attached to the rover by the time the mission ends.

Team: Lucas Gerges, Scarlett Khun Cho Lae, Orion North, and me.

Aircraft-tracking pointer

Summer 2026 – Present · Personal project

A desk device that physically points at nearby aircraft and shows their flight details. A Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W version alerts me whenever a plane flies within 5 km and displays its specs.

  • Pulls live ADS-B positions from the OpenSky Network API with Python, then sends pointing commands to an Arduino Uno over USB serial.
  • Shows live flight info on a 16x2 I2C LCD. Traced display failures to wiring, backlight, and library causes.
  • Set up Linux and Python on the Pi, and designed and 3D-printed the enclosure.
